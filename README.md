# GO-Chemistry-Desalination-MD
Molecular dynamics simulation data supporting the study "Influence of Oxygenated Functional Groups on Ion Rejection and Water Transport in Graphene Oxide Membranes". Includes input files, trajectories, and post-processing scripts for water flux and ion rejection analysis.


# Molecular Dynamics Data for Graphene Oxide Desalination Membranes

This repository contains the molecular dynamics (MD) simulation data and analysis scripts supporting the manuscript:

**"Influence of Oxygenated Functional Groups on Ion Rejection and Water Transport in Graphene Oxide Membranes: A Molecular Dynamics Study"**

##  Overview
The simulations investigate pressure-driven water transport and ion rejection in multilayer graphene oxide (GO) membranes with fixed geometry, focusing exclusively on the role of surface chemistry.

Membrane types include:
- GO
- Reduced GO (GOR)
- GO-OH
- GO-COOH
- Semi-functionalized membranes (GO-O$_{0.5}$, GO-OH$_{0.5}$, GO-COOH$_{0.25}$)

All simulations were performed using:
- **LAMMPS**
- **OPLS-AA force field**
- **TIP3P water model**
- Applied pressure: **300 atm**
